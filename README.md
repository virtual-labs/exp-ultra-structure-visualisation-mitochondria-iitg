### About the Experiment

This experiment uses transmission electron microscopy (TEM) to study the ultrastructure and visualise mitochondria in cancer cells at nanometre resolution.
A beam of high-energy electrons, typically accelerated between 40 and 200 kV, is transmitted through ultrathin sections of the biological specimen to generate high-contrast images.
Sample preparation involves fixation, dehydration, embedding, ultrathin sectioning and heavy-metal staining to preserve the mitochondrial architecture.
The images obtained allow detailed comparison of mitochondrial membranes, cristae organisation and matrix density between normal and cancerous cellular states.
